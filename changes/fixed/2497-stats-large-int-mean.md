- `stats.describe`, `stats.stats_mean`, `stats.median`, the variances and standard deviations, `stats.z_scores`, `stats.covariance`, `stats.correlation`, and `lab.status`, `lab.summary` and `lab.compare` no longer raise integer overflow on a list of large ints whose mean is representable: `stats.describe of ([5000000000000000000, 5000000000000000000])` has mean `5e+18`, as the builtin `mean` does. The sum stays exact while no step can overflow, so results on other lists are unchanged; `stats.stats_sum` still raises on int overflow (#2497).
