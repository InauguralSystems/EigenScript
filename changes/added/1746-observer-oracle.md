- `make observer-oracle` scores the observer against a corpus graded by mathematics (Hardy, NIST 6.4.4.2) and by stated conventions (numpy `isclose`, Keras `mode=min`). Its labels are derived from per-sequence properties. It reports agreement only and does not gate. On its first run the observer agreed with mathematics on 210 of 291 verdicts. An independent windowed reference model built from the textbook definitions scores 208 of 291 on the same inputs, so most misses are limits of a 10-step window. On that reference model, distance beats entropy as the signal, 208 to 195 (#1667).
